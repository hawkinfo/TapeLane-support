Preparing for the App Store

TapeLane is a native iPhone and iPad cassette player. The planned app price is US$4.99, paid once at download, with local prices set by the App Store. There is no app subscription. Apple Music requires its own subscription for full catalog playback and is not included with the app purchase.

Availability will exclude European Union member states and China mainland. This website does not sell the app or music.

Your own tapes

Import audio from Files, arrange tracks across Side A and Side B, and give each tape its own label.

Your collection

Browse Apple Music albums and playlists with permission. Keep a local shelf, export a backup, and restore missing tapes.

A quiet companion

Original cassette artwork, optional mechanical sounds, Home Screen widgets, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.

Support & questions

TapeLane is an independent app operated by hawkinfo. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. Apple Music availability depends on your account, subscription, region and network. The current app interface is in Simplified Chinese.
