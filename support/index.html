Open a support issue

GitHub issues are public. For a private request, use email instead. Do not post private audio, receipts, passwords or account details in a public issue. Never send passwords or verification codes by email.

System requirements

The upcoming compatibility build supports iOS 16.0 or later, including iOS 16.7.16, on iPhone and iPad. The previously prepared build requires iOS 17. This compatibility update has not been released on the App Store.

Try the player without an account

Open the app and press the orange Play key to hear the included original demo music. The sample tapes and imported local audio do not require Apple Music permission or a subscription. The current app interface is in Simplified Chinese.

Add your music

Tap + and choose local audio import to select files from Files. The app stores its own copies. For Apple Music, tap +, connect and grant music access. Browse your albums and playlists or search the catalog. Catalog playback requires an eligible Apple Music subscription and regional availability.

Arrange Side A and Side B

Open a tape's track list, then Edit. Turn on manual sides, choose how many tracks belong to Side A, and drag tracks into order. C30/C60/C90 are capacity guides: each side has half the selected total time. Over-capacity tracks are flagged but never cut off.

Back up and restore

Go to Preferences → Backup & widgets. Export a complete backup to Files. To restore, select the backup, review the verified counts, then choose Merge Restore. Existing tape IDs are skipped; missing tapes and their local audio are added. This is manual backup, not automatic cloud sync. Keep your backup safe; it is not encrypted by the app.

Widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and find TapeLane. Small and medium widgets are available. On iOS 17 or later, edit each widget to pin a tape. On iOS 16, choose a shared pinned tape in the app’s Preferences → 组件固定磁带; choose 最近播放 to follow recent playback. Tapping playback opens the app and starts the selected tape. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS, so the widget does not show continuous animation or a real-time progress counter.

When playback does not start

  1. Confirm that the demo tape can play.
  2. For Apple Music, check permission, subscription, network and the account's storefront. A song may not be available in every region.
  3. For imported files, confirm that the import completed. If a copy is missing or damaged, import the original again.
  4. Pause and try again after changing Bluetooth or AirPlay routes.

Purchases

The planned App Store price is US$4.99 paid once at download, with local equivalents managed by Apple. This app purchase does not include an Apple Music subscription. App Store purchase history, billing and refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, device model, iOS version, music source and steps to reproduce. Share only screenshots that do not reveal personal information.
