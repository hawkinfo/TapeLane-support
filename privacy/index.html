Privacy policy

Effective: November 16, 2026 · Contact details updated: November 20, 2026 · Applies to TapeLane 1.1 and this support website · 繁體中文摘要

The short version

TapeLane has no developer-operated accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. Your tape collection is stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives your imported audio, listening history or Apple Music library.

1. Who operates TapeLane

TapeLane is an independent iPhone and iPad app operated by hawkinfo. For questions about this policy, use the contact options on our support page.

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores tape names and notes, label colors, track order, Side A/B settings, capacity settings, track metadata, local audio copies, preferences and the last playback position in its local containers. This information is used to provide the player and collection features.

Home Screen widgets receive a small metadata snapshot through an App Group shared only by TapeLane and its widget extension on the same device. This can include tape titles, track titles, colors and the current side; it does not contain the audio files.

3. Imported audio and backups

When you select audio in Files, TapeLane copies it into its own local storage so playback can continue after access to the original provider ends. It does not move or delete your original file. Importing does not upload the copied audio to us.

If you export a backup, that file includes your tape metadata and imported local audio copies. You choose where to save it, including third-party storage or iCloud Drive. Backups are not encrypted by TapeLane. Apple Music backups contain identifiers and metadata, not Apple Music audio. Restore checks the archive and adds missing tapes without overwriting existing tape IDs.

TapeLane does not implement automatic cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, iOS may include app data in a device or iCloud backup. Storage providers and Apple handle such copies under their own policies.

4. Apple Music and network connections

Apple Music access is optional. After your permission, MusicKit communicates with Apple to browse your library, search the catalog, load artwork and play music. TapeLane keeps the identifiers and metadata needed for the tapes you create. It does not receive your Apple Account password, operate a music proxy, or send your music library to a developer server.

Music requests and artwork downloads communicate with Apple and its content delivery services, which may process network information under Apple's privacy policy. Full Apple Music catalog playback requires a separate eligible subscription. You can revoke music access in iOS Settings. Revoking permission does not automatically delete previously saved tape metadata; remove the relevant tapes in the app if you no longer want them stored.

5. Purchases and diagnostics

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. TapeLane does not collect payment-card or billing details. The app does not include advertising, third-party tracking, custom analytics or a third-party crash reporting SDK. Apple may process App Store, device backup and diagnostic information according to your account and device settings.

6. Support communications

If you contact us, we process the message and information you voluntarily provide to answer your request. GitHub support issues are public and are hosted by GitHub. Do not post passwords, private audio, receipts, account identifiers or other sensitive information in a public issue. For non-public support or privacy requests, email zifeit@163.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

7. Retention, deletion and choices

Local collection information remains until you edit or delete it or remove the app. Deleting an imported tape removes audio copies that are no longer referenced by another tape. Removing the app removes its local app storage as managed by iOS. Exported backups, copies held by Files providers, and device/iCloud backups must be managed separately by you. There is no TapeLane server account to delete.

You can use local audio without granting Apple Music access. Mechanical sounds, haptics and tape hiss can be turned off in Preferences. You can also remove widgets and revoke music permission through iOS.

8. This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add cookies, analytics scripts, embedded third-party media or web fonts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure its service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

9. Children and changes

TapeLane does not knowingly solicit children's personal information or create child profiles. Music content and account restrictions are provided by Apple and the selected audio sources. If the app's privacy practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated before or with the relevant release.
